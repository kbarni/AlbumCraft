AlbumCraft 0.6 can find pictures by what they show and propose tags for them, all on your computer. It also adds context menus for folders and pictures, eight web-gallery themes, and a check for new versions.

**New**
- **Smart search** (*Settings → AI features*): download a search pack once (Light, for searches in English, or Multilingual) and type what you are looking for, such as "beach at sunset" or "dog in the snow". The pictures that look like it follow the exact matches under *Looks like*. *More like this* in a picture's menu finds pictures similar to that one. Your pictures are analysed in the background; the status bar shows progress and a Pause button. Pictures and search text never leave your computer.
- **Tag proposals**: up to five tags proposed in the editor's Tags section and in the Tags dialog. They come from your own tags on similar pictures and from a built-in list of words in every language. Click one to add it. Right-click and choose *Not this tag* to stop seeing it for that picture.
- **Context menus** for folders (in the sidebar and on the grid header): select, slideshow, show on map, add to album, export, caption and description, open in the file manager, copy path, refresh thumbnails, rename, move, remove from the library, move to the trash. Pictures get open in the default program, *Open with*, copy path, rename, move to folder and move to the trash.
- **Rename and move** pictures and folders on disk, also to another disk (the copy is checked byte for byte before the original is removed). Notes, albums and captions follow, and Undo moves them back. What the trash refuses can be deleted for good, after a question.
- **Folder captions**: one line shown under the folder's name in the sidebar and the grid.
- **Left-out folders** (*Settings → Folders*): leave out sub-folders of a library folder.
- **Web gallery**: eight themes (paper, night, museum, darkroom, instant photos, ocean, vintage, blossom) chosen from previews made of your own pictures; square grid, column or justified layouts. The lightbox opens from the thumbnail and closes back into it, slides between pictures (arrows, drag or swipe), zooms with a click, the wheel or a pinch, and closes with Esc or a swipe down.
- **Updates**: at start, AlbumCraft asks GitHub whether there is a newer release (turn it off in *Settings → Interface*, or use *Check for Updates…* in the menu). On Linux (AppImage, `.deb`, Flatpak, `.tar.gz`) it can update itself and restart; on Windows and macOS it opens the release page.
- **MCP**: 49 tools, including `semantic_search`, `similar_pictures`, `propose_tags`, renaming, moving and trashing.

**Changes**
- The Linux packages are now built on Ubuntu 24.04 and need **Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13 or newer** (the AI features' ONNX Runtime needs glibc 2.38). The Flatpak works on older systems too.
- macOS comes as a `.dmg`; the Windows portable build is `-portable.zip`.
- The theme button left the top bar (it is in *Settings → Interface*); the Folders button opens *Settings → Folders*.

**Downloads**
- Windows x64: `albumcraft-0.6.0-windows-x64.msi` (installer) or `albumcraft-0.6.0-windows-x64-portable.zip`.
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.6.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz`.
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `albumcraft-0.6.0-macos-arm64.dmg`.

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- Face recognition is not there yet.
- Moved pictures are analysed again for smart search.
- *Open with* lists no programs on macOS.
- The MCP tools, the system's own error messages and EXIF values stay in English. Right-to-left languages are not supported.
- Video files are not shown or imported.
- Folders are still ordered by their newest file, also when the pictures are sorted by date taken.
- Printing doesn't work in the Flatpak yet. Use the `.deb` or the AppImage to print on Linux.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
