AlbumCraft 0.10.0 recognises people in your pictures, and the library's scroll bar is easier to grab.

**New**
- **Faces and people** (Settings › AI features, a small download of its own; nothing leaves your computer):
  - Faces are found in the background and grouped by person. Name a group once and the name follows every picture of that person.
  - People appear in the sidebar. Edit a person, merge or split groups, say *Not this person* for a wrong match, and choose a name from the drop-down of known people. A group is checked again after a split.
  - The editor shows face boxes on the picture (also in the zoomed view and over the edited picture), a People section, and *Add a face* for one the program missed.
  - **Delete all face data** removes everything the recogniser stored.
  - Names from `.picasa.ini` files and XMP face regions written by other programs are imported as named people, with their boxes. Written XMP and exported copies carry the typed and recognised names.
- **Library scroll bar**: the handle is wider and at least 72 pt tall, so it is easy to catch with the mouse. A second mode, *Speed* (the new default), keeps the handle in the middle of the track: drag it up or down and the library scrolls, faster the farther you drag; it springs back when let go. Settings › Interface › *Scroll bar of the library* switches back to *Position*.
- **Slideshow movies are made inside AlbumCraft**: the movie export no longer needs the FFmpeg program installed. Movies are MP4 files with MPEG-4 video and AAC sound (a free-licence encoder, so files are a little larger than H.264 ones); the music loops to fit.
- **Sidebar sections fold**: click the Albums, People, Tags or Folders heading to fold it away or open it again; AlbumCraft remembers which are folded.
- The editor opens on Basic or Advanced, whichever you used last.
- **For agents**: `list_people` and the face commands see imported faces; a command deletes all face data.

**Downloads**
- Windows x64: `albumcraft-0.10.0-windows-x64.msi` (installer) or `albumcraft-0.10.0-windows-x64-portable.zip`.
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.10.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz` (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer).
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `albumcraft-0.10.0-macos-arm64.dmg`.

Everything the app needs comes with it. Video playback and movie export use FFmpeg, which is included.

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- Colours are shown as sRGB: the monitor's own profile and printer profiles are not used yet. Adobe RGB pictures marked only in their EXIF, without a profile, are shown as sRGB.
- A date set in AlbumCraft is not written into the original file. Programs that read only the file, not the sidecar, still show the camera's date.
- XMP inside a file is changed only in JPEG and PNG files. For other files (HEIC and AVIF included), and for JPEG files with extended XMP (over 64 KB), the notes go into a sidecar file.
- Face regions written by other programs are kept as they are: a person removed in AlbumCraft but still named in a face region comes back at the next scan.
- Albums and folder notes are not written to XMP.
- Export, e-mail, web gallery, print, collage and movie use a video's thumbnail frame as a still picture; no trimmed copy of a video is made yet.
- Videos are decoded without the graphics card: 4K HEVC may not play smoothly on a slow computer.
- Live Photos and motion photos are not paired with their picture.
- Smart search and tag proposals look only at a video's thumbnail frame.
- Auto straighten can mistake one long roof or wall line seen at an angle for a horizon, and leaning natural shapes (trees, columns) for uprights. Undo or the Straighten slider puts it right.
- Faces: a person is found by the recogniser, so profiles, sunglasses, masks and small faces in a crowd can be missed or grouped apart; merge or split them by hand. Face data stays on this computer.
- Moved pictures are analysed again for smart search.
- *Open with* lists no programs on macOS.
- A `.tar.gz` copy updated by the built-in update keeps its old `libonnxruntime.so`.
- The MCP tools, the system's own error messages and EXIF values stay in English. Right-to-left languages are not supported.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
