AlbumCraft 0.8.0 brings videos into the library, next to your pictures.

**Changes**
- **Videos in the library**: MP4, MOV, 3GP, AVI, MKV / WebM, MTS / M2TS, MPG, DV and OGV files are shown in the grid with a play mark and their length. They sort by the date they were filmed, and the ones with a location show on the map. Videos filmed with the phone upright are shown upright.
- **Video player**: open a video to play it (click or Space). Click or drag on the filmstrip below it to move through the video, and step frame by frame with `,` and `.`. M turns the sound on and off.
- **In and out points**: mark the part to keep with `[` and `]`, the buttons, or by dragging the yellow marks on the filmstrip. The file itself is never changed. *Use this frame* picks the video's thumbnail.
- **Videos in slideshows**: a slideshow plays the marked part of each video and pauses the music while a clip with sound plays.
- **Editor**: the tools panel and the info panel can be resized by dragging their inner edge, and the list of edits has a height handle on top. The Advanced sliders take less room.
- **Histogram**: shows the picture's brightness by default, with the RGB curves one click away. Saturated colours (a deep blue sky) are no longer counted as clipped shadows.
- **Crop**: dragging a corner or edge now always takes the grip under the pointer.

**Downloads**
- Windows x64: `albumcraft-0.8.0-windows-x64.msi` (installer) or `albumcraft-0.8.0-windows-x64-portable.zip`.
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.8.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz` (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer).
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `albumcraft-0.8.0-macos-arm64.dmg`.

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- Export, e-mail, web gallery, print, collage and movie use a video's thumbnail frame as a still picture; no trimmed copy of a video is made yet.
- Videos are decoded without the graphics card: 4K HEVC may not play smoothly on a slow computer.
- Live Photos and motion photos are not paired with their picture.
- Smart search and tag proposals look only at a video's thumbnail frame.
- Auto straighten can mistake one long roof or wall line seen at an angle for a horizon, and leaning natural shapes (trees, columns) for uprights. Undo or the Straighten slider puts it right.
- Face recognition is not there yet.
- Moved pictures are analysed again for smart search.
- *Open with* lists no programs on macOS.
- A `.tar.gz` copy updated by the built-in update keeps its old `libonnxruntime.so`.
- The MCP tools, the system's own error messages and EXIF values stay in English. Right-to-left languages are not supported.
- Printing doesn't work in the Flatpak yet. Use the `.deb` or the AppImage to print on Linux.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
