AlbumCraft 0.4 makes collages and contact sheets, replaces the photo tray with a selection bar, and starts at once with large libraries. The editor keeps one adjustment and one crop per picture, with undo for every change.

**New**
- **Collages** in 9 styles (photo pile, mosaic, centre stage, grid, film strip, clothesline, fan, heart / circle / star, multiple exposure), with borders, shadows and a colour, gradient or picture background. Drag pictures to swap them, or move and turn them in a pile. Save as JPEG, PNG or PDF, or print.
- **Contact sheets**: pages of small pictures with a title, file names, captions or dates, page numbers, as PDF or printed.
- **Selection bar**: a single row under the grid, shown only while pictures are selected, with culling, rotate, tags, location, albums and everything you can make from them. In a narrow window its buttons lose their labels instead of overlapping.
- **Selecting**: drag on the background to select with a box (Ctrl or Shift adds to the selection), use the arrow keys (Shift+arrows to extend, Home / End, Space to open), and drag pictures onto an album in the sidebar to add them.
- With several pictures selected, the editor steps through only those, and the selection stays when you go back.
- **Sort** pictures by file name, date taken or rating.
- **Email**: small copies (1200 px) attached to a new message in your mail program: Thunderbird, Evolution or KMail on Linux, Outlook or Thunderbird on Windows, Mail on macOS. Where that isn't possible, AlbumCraft shows the folder with the copies to attach by hand.
- **Move rejected pictures to the trash**, from the menu or the rating filter, after a confirmation with their number and size. They go to the system's trash, so you can get them back until it is emptied. This is the only place where AlbumCraft removes picture files.
- **Editor**: one adjustment, one look, one straighten, one perspective and one crop per picture. The sliders keep their values after a crop, and steps no longer stack. Undo and Redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z) for every change, one step per slider movement; click a step in the list to go back to it.
- **Crop**: the tool shows the whole picture with its box; drag its edges, corners or inside. A fixed shape holds against the picture's edges; Enter applies. *Remove crop* can be undone.
- **Original**: hold the button or the `O` key to see the picture without your edits. This replaces the two-version before/after view. A double-click on the picture toggles Fit and 100 %.
- **Notices**: several at once; errors stay until you close them; results offer *Show in folder*. Deleting an album, removing a library folder and *Undo all edits* can be undone for 8 seconds.
- **Background work** (export, web gallery, movie, collage, email, trash) shows its progress in the status bar, with Cancel.
- Dialogs are modal: Esc or a click beside them cancels, Enter presses the main button of the simple ones.
- The menu is one short list instead of five menus.
- MCP: new commands `make_collage`, `make_contact_sheet`, `email_pictures`, `trash_rejected` (only rejected pictures, only with `confirm: true`) and `set_sort_order`.

**Faster**
- The library shows at once when AlbumCraft starts, from a catalogue of the last scan, while the folders are checked in the background. A library of 200,000 pictures reads in a third of a second.
- Only the rows on screen are laid out and drawn; the sidebar's counts and the search text are worked out once per change instead of on every frame.
- Large tiles and high-density screens get sharper 512 px thumbnails.
- Moving an editor slider re-applies only its own step and the later ones; straighten uses all cores.
- The date taken and the size of a picture are read in the background, never while the window waits.

**Fixes**
- Scanning again (also after an import) keeps the selection, the open picture and the loaded thumbnails, instead of closing the editor.
- Folders on a disk that is not connected show an empty library with *Scan again*, instead of sample pictures.
- Selecting many pictures (Ctrl+A on a large library) no longer slows every frame.
- A picture that can't be read is no longer asked for again and again; the editor says so.
- Back from the editor, the grid scrolls to the picture even when its row is off screen.
- A library saved by a newer version of AlbumCraft is never saved over (it would lose what the newer version added).
- Notes on the sample pictures are no longer written to the library.

**Downloads**
- Windows x86_64: `albumcraft-0.4.0-windows-x86_64.msi` (installer) or `.zip` (portable).
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.4.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz`.
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `AlbumCraft-0.4.0-macos-arm64.zip` (an app bundle).

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- Video files are not shown or imported.
- Email attaches the pictures only with the mail programs listed above. With others, and with webmail, attach the copies from the folder AlbumCraft shows.
- Folders are still ordered by their newest file, also when the pictures are sorted by date taken.
- In windows narrower than about 1000 pixels the editor's bottom bar gets crowded.
- Printing doesn't work in the Flatpak yet. Use the `.deb` or the AppImage to print on Linux.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
