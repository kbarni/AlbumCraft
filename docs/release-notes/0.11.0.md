AlbumCraft 0.11.0 shows your library as a timeline, lets you place and turn texts on a picture by hand, and lets you keep paintings and screenshots out of face recognition.

**New**
- **Timeline**: a new Timeline entry in the sidebar shows every picture by month, newest first (Settings › Folders can put the oldest first). Pictures dated only to the year (scanned prints) have their own group after that year's months, and pictures without a date come last with a *Set date…* button. Click a month's header to select its pictures.
- **Date scrubber** beside the grid, in the Timeline and in All pictures: years and months down the right edge, the part in view marked. Point at it to see the month, click or drag to go there. The arrow button above it hides or shows it, and AlbumCraft remembers your choice.
- **Pictures from a range of dates**: Shift+drag on the scrubber, or open the new *Dates* chip in the top bar (one bar per month, the years as buttons) to show only the pictures taken then. Click the chip to change the dates or show every date again.
- **Texts on the picture**: with a text chosen in the editor's Effects tab, drag it to move it, pull its square corner to resize it and its round corner to turn it (it snaps to level and to 15° steps). Texts can use any font installed on your computer, in bold and italic.
- **Leave pictures out of face detection**: right-click pictures or a folder and choose *Leave out of face detection* for paintings, posters or screenshots of games. Their faces are not looked for and no longer appear with the people. *Look for faces again* undoes it, and Settings › AI features lists what is left out.
- **For agents**: `search_photos` takes `taken_from` / `taken_to`, and `exclude_from_face_detection` keeps pictures and folders out of face detection.

**Downloads**
- Windows x64: `albumcraft-0.11.0-windows-x64.msi` (installer) or `albumcraft-0.11.0-windows-x64-portable.zip`.
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.11.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz` (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer).
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `albumcraft-0.11.0-macos-arm64.dmg`.

Everything the app needs comes with it. Video playback and movie export use FFmpeg, which is included.

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- Colours are shown as sRGB: the monitor's own profile and printer profiles are not used yet. Adobe RGB pictures marked only in their EXIF, without a profile, are shown as sRGB.
- A date set in AlbumCraft is not written into the original file. Programs that read only the file, not the sidecar, still show the camera's date.
- XMP inside a file is changed only in JPEG and PNG files. For other files (HEIC and AVIF included), and for JPEG files with extended XMP (over 64 KB), the notes go into a sidecar file.
- Face regions written by other programs are kept as they are: a person removed in AlbumCraft but still named in a face region comes back at the next scan.
- Albums and folder notes are not written to XMP.
- Export, e-mail, web gallery, print, collage and movie use a video's thumbnail frame as a still picture; no trimmed copy of a video is made yet.
- Videos are decoded without the graphics card: 4K HEVC may not play smoothly on a slow computer.
- Live Photos and motion photos are not paired with their picture.
- Smart search and tag proposals look only at a video's thumbnail frame.
- Auto straighten can mistake one long roof or wall line seen at an angle for a horizon, and leaning natural shapes (trees, columns) for uprights. Undo or the Straighten slider puts it right.
- Faces: a person is found by the recogniser, so profiles, sunglasses, masks and small faces in a crowd can be missed or grouped apart; merge or split them by hand. Face data stays on this computer.
- Faces found in a picture before it was left out of face detection stay in the face data on this computer (hidden from the people, removed by *Delete all face data*).
- Moved pictures are analysed again for smart search.
- *Open with* lists no programs on macOS.
- A `.tar.gz` copy updated by the built-in update keeps its old `libonnxruntime.so`.
- The MCP tools, the system's own error messages and EXIF values stay in English. Right-to-left languages are not supported.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
