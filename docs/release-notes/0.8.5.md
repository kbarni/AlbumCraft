AlbumCraft 0.8.5 shares its notes with other photo programs through XMP.

**Changes**
- **XMP read at every scan**: ratings, rejects, captions, tags (with their hierarchy), people and locations that other programs keep in XMP are brought in, whether it is inside the picture (JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF and raw files) or in a sidecar file next to it (`photo.jpg.xmp` or `photo.xmp`). The first time, AlbumCraft only fills in what it doesn't have yet, so your own notes are never overwritten. After that, a field changed in another program replaces AlbumCraft's, field by field.
- **Write notes to XMP**: a new item in a picture's menu writes the rating, pick or reject, caption, tags, people, the location you set and the edits into the pictures' XMP. Nothing is written unless you ask. The notes go into the sidecar file when there is one, otherwise into the XMP already inside a JPEG or PNG file, otherwise into a new sidecar file. Everything else in the XMP is kept as it was. Settings › Folders › XMP can stop AlbumCraft from changing the XMP inside picture files, so that only sidecar files are written.
- **Exported and emailed copies carry XMP**: rating, caption, tags and people, and the location when the export includes it. The new *Notes (XMP)* switch in the Export view and in Settings › Export leaves them out.
- **For agents**: a new `write_xmp` command, and `include_notes` for `export`.

**Downloads**
- Windows x64: `albumcraft-0.8.5-windows-x64.msi` (installer) or `albumcraft-0.8.5-windows-x64-portable.zip`.
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.8.5-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz` (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer).
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `albumcraft-0.8.5-macos-arm64.dmg`.

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- XMP inside a file is changed only in JPEG and PNG files; for other files, and for JPEG files with extended XMP (over 64 KB), the notes go into a sidecar file.
- Face regions written by other programs are kept as they are: a person removed in AlbumCraft but still named in a face region comes back at the next scan.
- Albums and folder notes are not written to XMP.
- Errors shown when writing XMP fails are in English.
- Export, e-mail, web gallery, print, collage and movie use a video's thumbnail frame as a still picture; no trimmed copy of a video is made yet.
- Videos are decoded without the graphics card: 4K HEVC may not play smoothly on a slow computer.
- Live Photos and motion photos are not paired with their picture.
- Smart search and tag proposals look only at a video's thumbnail frame.
- Auto straighten can mistake one long roof or wall line seen at an angle for a horizon, and leaning natural shapes (trees, columns) for uprights. Undo or the Straighten slider puts it right.
- Face recognition is not there yet.
- Moved pictures are analysed again for smart search.
- *Open with* lists no programs on macOS.
- A `.tar.gz` copy updated by the built-in update keeps its old `libonnxruntime.so`.
- The MCP tools, the system's own error messages and EXIF values stay in English. Right-to-left languages are not supported.
- Printing doesn't work in the Flatpak yet. Use the `.deb` or the AppImage to print on Linux.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
