AlbumCraft 0.5 speaks your language: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese (Brazil). It also brings a Settings dialog, a new Export view with signatures and copyright, and a full-screen viewer.

**New**
- **Interface languages**: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese (Brazil). AlbumCraft follows the system's language; choose another in *Settings → Interface*, and it changes at once. Dates, numbers and file sizes follow the language too, as do the web gallery's page and the contact sheet. The desktop entry and the software-centre description are translated as well.
- **Settings** (from the menu, or Ctrl+,): the sections are listed on the left. They cover the interface (language, theme, the bottom bar, picture size), library folders, editing, slideshows, export defaults, maps and storage (notes file, thumbnail cache). Changes are saved at once.
- **Export** is now a full view, like Import: the options are on the left and the pictures on the right. Click a picture to leave it out. Add a text or logo **signature** (position, size, opacity, colour, shadow), shown on every thumbnail before you export. Write the **creator and copyright** into the copies, and choose whether they keep the camera information (EXIF) and the location. The folder is remembered.
- **Full-screen viewer** from the editor (its button or F11): the pictures from the current one, with music if you choose a file. F11 or Esc leaves.
- **Slideshows** can wait for you instead of moving on by themselves.
- **Bottom bar**: choose the compact selection bar or the tray, which is always shown and has thumbnails of the selection.
- **Scrolling** in the library: faster with the wheel, and you can drag with the middle button or a finger. Two-finger touchpad scrolling keeps gliding after your fingers stop.

**Fixes**
- Email: attachments whose names held a comma were not attached in Thunderbird. The copies now get safe names.
- Longer languages fit in the smallest window: the top bar and the editor's bottom bar make room instead of overlapping.
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean text uses a font of the system (Noto CJK, or the Windows and macOS ones).

**Downloads**
- Windows x86_64: `albumcraft-0.5.0-windows-x86_64.msi` (installer) or `.zip` (portable).
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.5.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz`.
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `AlbumCraft-0.5.0-macos-arm64.zip` (an app bundle).

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- The MCP tools, the system's own error messages and EXIF values stay in English. Right-to-left languages are not supported.
- Video files are not shown or imported.
- Email attaches the pictures only with Thunderbird, Evolution, KMail, Outlook and Apple Mail. With other mail programs, and with webmail, attach the copies from the folder AlbumCraft shows.
- Folders are still ordered by their newest file, also when the pictures are sorted by date taken.
- Printing doesn't work in the Flatpak yet. Use the `.deb` or the AppImage to print on Linux.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
