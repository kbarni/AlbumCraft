AlbumCraft 0.2 adds **Places**, a map of where your pictures were taken, along with quicker culling and new geometry tools in the editor.

**New**
- **Places**: a map of your pictures, with nearby photos grouped into markers. GPS positions from phones and cameras are read automatically, and the original files are never written.
- **Set location**: search the built-in list of 34,000 cities, type coordinates, or click and drag a pin, then name the place. Exported copies can carry the location in their EXIF (a checkbox in the Export window). Library search also matches place names.
- A built-in world map is always available. OpenStreetMap tiles and address search are used only if you agree; you're asked once at the first start, and the setting can be changed on the map and in Tools.
- **Culling from the grid**: hover a picture to reject it or click one of five stars. The photo tray also has reject and rating buttons next to pick.
- **Editor geometry** (Advanced › Geometry): a straighten slider plus vertical and horizontal perspective correction.
- **Zoom by dragging**: click and drag on the fitted picture to zoom smoothly to 100 % around that point and keep panning.
- MCP: new commands `set_location`, `remove_location` and `find_place`. `search_photos` can now filter by `has_location` and `near`.

**Fixes and improvements**
- Icons in menus and buttons (☰, ★, ⚑, …) no longer show as empty boxes on Windows.
- The library is saved in the background, in a more compact file, so large libraries no longer pause the window when saving.
- The "Edits on this picture" list keeps room for six steps instead of shrinking to one line.
- If a folder scan or a whole export fails unexpectedly, you now get a message instead of nothing happening.
- Internally, the code is split into an edit pipeline, a core library and the app, a first step toward running the library on a home server.

**Downloads**
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.2.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` (binary, icon, `.desktop` file).
- Windows x86_64: `albumcraft-0.2.0-windows-x86_64.zip`.
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `AlbumCraft-0.2.0-macos-arm64.zip` (an app bundle).

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- Perspective correction is a simple keystone, and no guide lines are drawn while you move the geometry sliders.
- The map always shows the whole library rather than following the current album or filter, and its built-in map goes up to zoom level 13.
- Printing assumes 5 mm margins on Linux and macOS.
- Movie export needs FFmpeg on the `PATH`.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
