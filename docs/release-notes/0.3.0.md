AlbumCraft 0.3 separates importing from adding a folder, finds pictures by place, and stays fast with large folders on external disks. The editor gets an info panel and better geometry tools.

**New**
- **Import** from a memory card, a camera or any folder. Pictures are grouped by time, with a checkbox per group; the ones already in your library (same content, even renamed) start hidden; a large preview shows the picture you click. The checked pictures are copied into a new folder you name, in the background, and the folder opens when it's done. Nothing is ever overwritten, identical files are skipped, and the originals stay as they are.
- **Folders** in the top bar opens the library's folder list. Adding a folder there copies nothing: the pictures are shown where they are.
- **One search box** for words in names and captions, tags, people, places, albums and folders, with suggestions as you type and their picture counts. A chosen tag, place or person becomes a chip in the box; chips combine. Places are found by city, region or country, also for pictures that only carry the camera's GPS position.
- **Info panel** in the editor (Info button or `I`), in sections you can fold: properties, extended EXIF, location with a small map, and tags with suggestions from your library.
- **Show on map** for a folder (map button on its header) or an album (its menu): Places shows only those pictures.
- **Editor geometry**: crop, straighten and perspective now come first in the Advanced tab, with an alignment grid on the picture while you move the sliders. Crop has more shapes (original, square, 4 × 3, 3 × 2, 16 × 9), landscape or portrait orientation, starts with the largest box of the chosen shape and can be moved by dragging.
- **One icon set** everywhere (Material Symbols), drawn the same on Windows, macOS and Linux instead of each system's emoji.
- In the photo tray, the button next to Tags sets the location of the pictures.
- MCP: new commands `import_pictures`, `rescan` and `show_on_map`; `search_photos` can filter by `tag` and `place`.

**Faster**
- The library no longer freezes while scrolling through folders on a slow or external disk: picture shapes are read in the background, and only the start of each file.
- Thumbnails come from the preview the camera stores in each picture: about 30 times faster for phone pictures (886 thumbnails in under a second), and less than a tenth of the data read from the disk.
- Raw files and large previews open faster; at most 1200 thumbnails are kept in memory.
- The picture you are looking at is always loaded first, then what is on screen.

**Fixes**
- A folder added inside another library folder no longer shows its pictures twice.

**Downloads**
- Windows x86_64: `albumcraft-0.3.0-windows-x86_64.msi` (installer) or `.zip` (portable).
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.3.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz`.
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `AlbumCraft-0.3.0-macos-arm64.zip` (an app bundle).

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- Video files are not shown or imported.
- The crop box can't be resized by its corners yet (draw a new one), and *Remove crop* can't be undone.
- In windows narrower than about 1000 pixels, the photo tray and the editor's bottom bar get crowded.
- Printing doesn't work in the Flatpak yet. Use the `.deb` or the AppImage to print on Linux.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
