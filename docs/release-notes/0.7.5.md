AlbumCraft 0.7.5 is a test release of the reworked editor: the edit list is now a recipe in a fixed order, and the one-click fixes set the sliders instead of adding steps of their own. Please try it on your own pictures and report what looks wrong.

**Changes**
- **Edits in a fixed order**: turn, straighten, perspective, crop, red-eye, adjustment, look, then vignette, rounded corners, border, text and shadow. A new edit goes to its place whatever adds it (the panels, pasted edits, the MCP commands). A picture has one vignette, border, shadow and rounding; adding one changes the one it has. Earlier edit lists are kept as they are.
- **Click a step to change it**: a click on a step in the editor's list brings back its settings (the crop tool, the sliders, the look or the effect), and the × beside it removes only that step.
- **One-click fixes set the sliders**: *Auto exposure*, *Auto contrast*, *Auto colour* and *I'm feeling lucky* now move the Advanced sliders (exposure, shadows, black point, contrast, white balance, vibrance), so you can see and fine-tune what they did. They try settings on the picture itself to reach the right brightness and black and white points.
- **Careful white balance**: the automatic white balance goes by the nearly gray parts of the picture, only part of the way, and leaves a picture without gray parts alone.
- **Auto straighten with perspective**: it finds the upright lines of buildings and corrects the angle and the vertical perspective together, or else levels a horizon (up to 5°). A half turn is now its own step.

**Downloads**
- Windows x64: `albumcraft-0.7.5-windows-x64.msi` (installer) or `albumcraft-0.7.5-windows-x64-portable.zip`.
- Linux x86_64: `albumcraft-0.7.5-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, `.deb`, `.flatpak` or `.tar.gz` (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer).
- macOS (Apple Silicon): `albumcraft-0.7.5-macos-arm64.dmg`.

These builds are not signed. On macOS, right-click the app and choose *Open* the first time. On Windows, choose *More info* → *Run anyway* in the SmartScreen prompt.

**Known limitations**
- Auto straighten can mistake one long roof or wall line seen at an angle for a horizon, and leaning natural shapes (trees, columns) for uprights. Undo or the Straighten slider puts it right.
- The horizontal perspective is never set automatically.
- Face recognition is not there yet.
- Moved pictures are analysed again for smart search.
- *Open with* lists no programs on macOS.
- A `.tar.gz` copy updated by the built-in update keeps its old `libonnxruntime.so`.
- The MCP tools, the system's own error messages and EXIF values stay in English. Right-to-left languages are not supported.
- Video files are not shown or imported.
- Printing doesn't work in the Flatpak yet. Use the `.deb` or the AppImage to print on Linux.
- Raw files are not developed from sensor data; edits work on the embedded preview.
